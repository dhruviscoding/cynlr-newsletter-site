SRSwiss Robotics Day

CynLr's booth at Swiss Robotics Day — engineers stopping to see the demo live.

A pen cap is harder to handle than it looks

On November 1st, CynLr took part in Swiss Robotics Day — a flagship project of the Swiss National Centre of Competence in Research (NCCR) Robotics, with EPFL as the leading house and ETH Zurich co-leading.

We demonstrated something deceptively simple: pressing a cap onto a pen and closing it. It takes exact force control, sensing the counter-pressure of the other hand through the object, and synchronizing movement in response — dynamics most robots today still can't replicate.

Most vision systems are trained once and expect the world to hold still. Ours doesn't get that luxury — orientation, grip, and force all shift mid-task, which is why CyRo leans on real-time force feedback instead of retraining for every new object. The engineers in the room noticed. CynLr plans to keep working with that community over the next two quarters.
